## What the decision rule can resolve

For one stage, support requires at least 15 of 20 differences strictly below `-log10(2)`; exclusion requires at least 15 strictly above it. Equality at the margin helps neither direction. Both stages must independently give the same category, and any SVF/IVF disables confirmation in either direction.

Under the declared iid/stationarity model, let `p=P(D < -log10(2))`. The probability of support in both stages is `[sum(k=15..20) binom(20,k) p^k (1-p)^(20-k)]^2`. It is approximately 0.173 at p=0.70 and 0.647 at p=0.80. These are hypothetical design calculations, not estimated power or a prediction. Exclusion uses the separate probability `q=P(D > -log10(2))`; substituting `1-p` requires no mass at the margin.
